THE SIX DECISIONS

Same plans.
Six decisions.

The product stays the same. The presentation removes friction one decision at a time.

01

Hierarchy — The price speaks first.

Use a bold 52px price, while plan labels and supporting details stay quiet and compact.

52px PRICE
02

Spacing — Room to compare.

Give three cards room to breathe with 20px gaps, 24px padding, and clear 14px feature rows.

20 / 24 / 14
03

Emphasis — One plan to pick.

Make one recommended plan obvious with an accent border, a badge, and the only filled button.

ONE DEFAULT
04

Depth — Lift the pick.

Raise the featured card 16px with a soft shadow and a slightly stronger edge so it feels like the front choice.

16px LIFT
05

Anchor — Show the saving.

Make yearly billing the default discount view, show the monthly price struck through, and state the saving clearly.

−20% YEARLY
06

Motion — Every click answers.

Hover lifts the plan, billing rolls the price, and pressing confirms the action with clear feedback.

100ms / 300ms
RULEOne recommended plan. One filled button. Every action gives feedback.
